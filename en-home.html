Frequently asked questions

What people usually ask us

How much does the valuation of my home cost?

Nothing. The valuation is free, with no obligation to sell.

Is the valuation an official appraisal?

No. It is a reasoned market valuation. Appraisals valid for mortgage purposes can only be issued by approved valuation companies.

Which documents do I need to sell?

Among others, the title deed, the land registry extract, the energy certificate, the certificate of occupancy and the latest property tax (IBI) receipt. We help you gather them; the full list is on our blog. The documents you need to sell your home

What taxes and costs are involved in selling a home?

Mainly the municipal capital gains tax (plusvalía) and income tax on the gain, plus cancelling the mortgage if there is one. We explain it in detail on our blog. Costs and taxes when selling a flat in Catalonia

Is the energy certificate compulsory to sell or let?

Yes. You must have it before advertising the property, and its rating must appear in the adverts.

Spanish, Catalan and English.
